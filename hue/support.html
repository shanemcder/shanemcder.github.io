Support

Questions about Hue? Here's how to get started and solve common problems.

How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the screen to cycle through colors. Release when the blob matches the target color. Keep matching to increase your score.

How is my best score saved?

Your personal best is saved on your device. Removing the app may remove locally stored game progress.

The game isn't working as expected

Try closing and reopening Hue, then make sure you're running an up-to-date version of the app and iOS. If the issue persists, contact support with your iPhone model, iOS version, and a description of the problem.
