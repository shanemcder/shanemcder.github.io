Privacy Policy

Hue is designed to be a simple, enjoyable color-matching game. This policy explains how information is handled.

Information in the game

Hue stores gameplay preferences and your personal best score locally on your device. This information is used to provide the game's features.

Personal information and tracking

Hue does not require an account. The game is not intended to collect personal information or track your activity across other companies' apps or websites.

Advertising and analytics

Hue does not include advertising or third-party analytics in its current version.

Sharing and retention

Hue does not transmit your game score to a developer-operated server. Locally saved game information remains on your device unless you remove it or uninstall the app, subject to iOS backup and device behavior.

Children's privacy

Hue does not ask players to provide personal information or create an account.

Changes to this policy

If Hue's features or data practices change, this page will be updated to reflect those changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer.
