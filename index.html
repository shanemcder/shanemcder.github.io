October 2026 · iOS Game Development

Hue (app)

Hue is an iPhone game I built with SwiftUI. I had the idea for this game for a while now. The object is to match the color in the middle of the screen to the name of the color appearing on screen. As you hold the screen down, the color in the middle morphs through the spectrum. Release once you match the color to move on to the next level.

As you progress, a more diverse set of color names unlocks. There are milestone animations at levels 10, 20, 30, and so on, and the visuals become increasingly dreamlike. I wanted to make not only a fun game, but also something captivating and entrancing—a relaxing little brain break.

I also think Hue is a great learning tool for kids discovering new colors.

One of the hardest parts of the project was learning to use Metal shaders in Xcode to get the central colored blob looking right.

Next, I want to add music and continue improving the visuals.

Will be on the app store soon!

Current · Full-Stack Development

TestPressing.net

TestPressing is a web platform for music creators to share works-in-progress, get feedback, and showcase their audio work. Built with React and TypeScript, it includes audio uploads and playback, public portfolios with personal links, friends, and activity updates.

Account features include login and signup, email verification, password recovery, and password changes. The platform is still in early development, and the backend occasionally experiences downtime as I work on its reliability.

The idea grew out of a portfolio website I built for the audio work I create in my free time. I’m also part of audio engineering Discord communities, where people typically share demos by uploading clips directly into the chat. Those clips get buried quickly, and sharing unfinished ideas can feel uncomfortable when you don’t know or trust everyone in the server.

I wanted a more intentional way to share work with others, while giving audio engineers a place to showcase their strongest moments without asking someone to listen through an entire track. That’s why TestPressing combines sharing works-in-progress with a public portfolio that creators can send through a personal link.

I also built it to encourage creativity and help artists finish what they start. In my own experience, putting a demo out there can be what moves it forward. Listening to something you’ve shared often makes you realize it doesn’t sound as unfinished as you thought, while feedback from friends helps you figure out where to take it next.

The minimalistic design is intentional: I wanted the main focus to be around the vinyls displayed on each profile to intrigue the viewer, prompting them to click on them and hear the music. Users can connect their soundcloud link in order to garner more followers.

Working on getting 100 users signed up.

React · TypeScript · Audio Playback · Authentication · Public Portfolios

October 2026 · iOS App Development

TextShield

TextShield is an iPhone message-filtering app I built with Swift and SwiftUI to help users filter unwanted text messages. Users can maintain a custom list of blocked words and filter messages from unknown senders that contain those terms.

The app uses Apple's message filtering extension to evaluate incoming messages, with a simple interface for managing filter rules. I developed and tested it on an iPhone and am exploring distribution through TestFlight.
